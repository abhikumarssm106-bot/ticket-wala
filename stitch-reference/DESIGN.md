## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, reliability, and precision of a high-touch personal concierge tailored for the Indian travel and events landscape. The service bridges the gap between chaotic self-service booking engines and the peace of mind of a dedicated human assistant.

The aesthetic marries **Corporate / Modern** clarity with **Human-Centered Utility**:
- **Tone:** Grounded, accountable, swift, and respectful. No bloated promotional fluff or deceptive dark patterns.
- **Visual Stance:** Crisp white surfaces, crisp structural lines, and purposeful contrast. It delivers the reassuring gravitas of an established institutional service combined with the direct accessibility of neighborhood commerce.
- **Tactility:** Restrained, functional interactive states. Subtle elevation shifts signal availability and action without gimmicky blur filters or oversized AI-generated gradients.
